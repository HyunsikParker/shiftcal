1 Paste your shifts

One date and shift per line. Korean or English. Leave out names, workplace details and other people’s shifts.

Text only. No uploads, accounts or calendar permissions.

Month & shift times

Times use Korea Standard Time (UTC+09:00). These are example hours; change them to match your workplace.

ShiftStartsEnds

Use 24-hour times (HH:MM). An end time earlier than its start means the following day.

What stays on your computer?

In local mode, text goes only to your local Gemma process. ShiftCal does not store your roster or use analytics. The public demo replays a saved model response to a fictional example; it cannot read a new roster. Refresh or use Clear to discard your session.
